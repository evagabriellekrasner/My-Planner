# My Planner

Keep your classes, homework, tests and activities in one place, and see exactly what to do today.

**[Open My Planner](https://evagabriellekrasner.github.io/My-Planner/)**

It runs in your web browser on any phone or computer. There's nothing to pay for and no email needed.

## Get started

1. Open My Planner and tap **New planner**.
2. Pick your name and a 4-number passcode.
3. Add your classes.
4. Add your homework and tests, or import them from a document.

To open your planner on another device, log in with the same name and passcode.

> **Keep your planner safe:** a 4-number passcode is easy to guess, so don't put private information in your planner. Don't use a passcode you use anywhere else.

## What you can do

- **Today:** see your tests, homework and to-dos for the day, with the most urgent first. Add your own to-dos too.
- **Classes:** add homework, tests, quizzes, projects and reading to each class.
- **Import:** drop in a PDF or paste text from a teacher's weekly plan. You check everything before it's added.
- **Calendar:** see it all by month, and add sports, practices, appointments and other events, including ones that repeat.
- **School schedule:** set up A/B days or a rotating schedule, and mark days off. Then **Next class** can pick a due date for you.
- **Study:** pick a test and how many days you have, and get a day-by-day study plan.
- **Reminders:** get a morning summary and reminders for events on your phone.
- **Settings:** choose your colors, dark mode and app icon.

## Put it on your phone

Adding My Planner to your home screen makes it open like a regular app.

**iPhone or iPad:** open it in Safari, tap the Share button, then tap **Add to Home Screen**.

**Android:** open it in Chrome, tap the ⋮ menu, then tap **Add to Home screen** or **Install app**.

## Turn on reminders

1. On iPhone or iPad, add the app to your home screen first and open it from there.
2. Go to **Settings** and turn on notifications.
3. Tap **Allow** when your phone asks.
4. Send yourself a test reminder to check that it works.

Reminders can arrive a few minutes late.

## Good to know

- **You need the internet.** My Planner doesn't work offline.
- **Your planner is saved online,** so it follows you to any device where you log in.
- **Use one device at a time.** If you have My Planner open in two places, reload before making changes so older information doesn't replace your newer work.
- **Save a backup now and then.** Go to **Settings** to save a backup file, which you can load again later.
- **Don't forget your passcode.** There's no way to reset it, so write it down somewhere safe.

## Having trouble?

**It won't load on school Wi-Fi.** Some school networks block the database My Planner uses. Try another network or your phone's data.

**My PDF import found nothing.** Scanned documents can't be read, because the PDF needs real text in it. Try copying the text and pasting it in instead.

**I'm not getting reminders.** Check that notifications are on in **Settings** and allowed for the app in your phone's settings. On iPhone, the app must be opened from your home screen.

**My planner won't open with my passcode.** Check that your name is spelled the same way as when you made the planner (capital letters don't matter), then check the passcode. If you changed your passcode on another device, use the new one.
